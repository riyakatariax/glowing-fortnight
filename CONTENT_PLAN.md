# Portfolio website: content plan

A working plan for Riya Kataria's portfolio site, based on the four versions of the CV
(Comms, Entertainment/Music, Business, Politics).

## Positioning

All four CVs tell the same story: **someone who makes people pay attention, whether the
subject is a Congressman, a pop star, a queer arts nonprofit or a couple on their wedding day.**
The site should say that once, clearly, then let each type of recruiter find their own
evidence.

Draft homepage line (pick one, or rewrite):

- "I tell stories for artists, causes and brands, in words, pictures and strategy."
- "Communications strategist, music journalist and photographer."
- "From Capitol Hill press releases to Chappell Roan: communications that people notice."

Sub-line: Oxford PPE · LSE MSc Media & Communications (Distinction) · LBS MiM (Merit Scholar).
U.S. citizen, based in [city], open to comms, marketing and entertainment roles.

## Case studies (the most important section)

Recruiters skim galleries but read outcomes. Each case study: 3 to 5 sentences plus one or two
visuals. Format: **the problem → what you did → the result**.

1. **Portland Queer Arts Project: 500% follower growth.** Multi-channel strategy, Google/Meta
   ads, A/B testing, 20+ performers, new sponsors. Show before/after follower screenshots and
   2 to 3 posts you made.
2. **Rep. Ro Khanna's office: press and rapid response.** 15+ press communications, letters
   to President Biden on inflation and foreign policy, rapid-response social for a 1.2M+
   following. Link the public releases; add your Hill photos here too.
3. **CRESCENDO Magazine: U.S. coordinator.** Artist relations with Chappell Roan, Rebecca
   Black, Remi Wolf, Lola Young. Concert photos, pull quotes from reviews, links to published
   pieces.
4. **Oxford Taylor Swift Society: building a fandom from zero.** Fastest-growing society,
   100K+ organic impressions, 5,000+ attendees, coverage in The Times. Show the Times clipping
   and the graphics you made (this covers the "I haven't done graphics lately" gap).
5. **Riya Kataria Photography: founding a business.** Clients in 10+ countries, self-built
   website, SEO, branding. This frames the wedding work as entrepreneurship, not a hobby.
6. **LBS Out in Business / EurOut.** Social strategy for Europe's largest LGBTQ+ student
   conference across 30+ universities. Show campaign posts.
7. *(Optional, entertainment roles)* **OxRecords: student record label.** A&R, artist branding
   for 10+ artists, industry workshops.

## Things to fix before launch

The CVs don't agree on a few details. The site puts everything in one place, and recruiters
will check it against LinkedIn, so pick one version of each:

- **Phone number:** the Comms CV has +1 (510) 458 9805; the other three have +1 (408) 475 9003.
- **LBS dates:** "Aug 2024 – Jul 2025" on three CVs, "Sep 2024 – Aug 2025" on the
  Entertainment one.
- **LBS degree name:** "MSc in Management" vs "Masters in Management".
- **St. Catherine's VP end date:** Dec 2022 vs Jan 2023.

## Notes and cautions

- **Fan edits:** they use copyrighted TV footage and music, so keep them on YouTube/TikTok and
  embed from there instead of hosting the files. Present them as editing work (pacing,
  cutting to music). They help most for entertainment and social roles. Leave them off the
  site version you send to political offices.
- **Congress photos:** only post material that was already public or that you're sure is fine
  to share. When in doubt, ask the office.
- **Wedding photos:** get permission from the couples if you don't have it already.
- **Tailoring:** since you apply across four fields, the Work page filters let a politics
  recruiter and a record label each find their own case studies on the same site.
  Put the site URL at the top of every CV.
